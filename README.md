# Hindu Rituals Guide

Welcome to this educational resource about traditional Hindu religious practices and ancestral rituals.

## Pind Daan Puja

Pind Daan is a traditional Hindu ritual performed to honour departed ancestors through symbolic offerings and prayers. It is commonly associated with Shradh and other ancestral observances.

Learn about its meaning, traditional procedure and religious significance in this [Pind Daan Puja guide by RitualPuja](https://www.ritualpuja.com/pujas/pind-daan-puja).

## Narayan Bali Puja

Narayan Bali is a traditional ceremony associated with Lord Vishnu and specific rites for the spiritual welfare of departed souls.

## Shradh and Pitru Paksha

Shradh and Pitru Paksha are important parts of Hindu ancestral traditions. Families observe these practices according to their regional customs and religious guidance.

## About This Repository

This repository shares general educational information about Hindu rituals and Indian spiritual traditions. Ritual procedures may vary by family and region.
